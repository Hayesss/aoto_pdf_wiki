distinct subtypes. We simultaneously profiled 53,417 hematopoietic cells and predicted their interactions
with non-hematopoietic subsets. We employed co-detection by indexing (CODEX) to spatially profile over
1.2millioncells.WeintegratedscRNA-seqandCODEXdatatolinkpredictedcellularsignalingwithspatial
