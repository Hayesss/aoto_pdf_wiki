42 The mammalian nucleolus is spatially organized into four nested layers2,10, each
43 plays a distinct role in ribosome production1,10. In mammals, 18S, 5.8S and 28S
44 ribosomal RNAs (rRNAs) are transcribed as a single 47S pre-rRNA, approximately 13
2
45 kb in length11. Transcription by RNA polymerase I (Pol I) occurs at the FC/DFC
46 border12,13, after which the 47S pre-rRNA moves outward across each nucleolar layer,
W
47 ultimately forming SSU3,4 and LSU5 in the GC1,14. The major precursor of SSU pre-
48 rRNA is 30S, flanked by the 5' external and internal transcribed spacers (ETS and ITS1), E
49 while the LSU precursor is mainly 32S pre-rRNA, producing 5.8S and 28S rRNAsI
V
50 (Extended Data Fig. 1a). Given that the 47S pre-rRNA would span roughly 4~10 μm if
E
51 fully extended15, it must fold considerably to fit within the much smaller nucleolus,
R
52 where the FC region measures around 150 nm and the FC-DFC region is ~400 nm in
P
53 diameter2,12. This suggests that pre-rRNAs and interacting protein s undergo substantial
E
54 compaction during ribosome assembly.
L
C
55 Specifically, nascent pre-rRNA is translocated to the DFC, where it undergoes
I
56 chemical modifications and early cleavageTs at 5' and 3' ends12,16. Classic models
R
57 propose that subsequent processing in the GC removes ETSs and ITSs to produce SSU
A
58 and LSU6,7 (Fig. 1a; Extended Data Fig. 1a). Although supported by extensive
59 correlative evidence over decDades, in situ studies have remained limited due to technical
60 constraints inherent to Econventional imaging and biochemical approaches6,7,11. Our
61 recent imaging studTies revealed a spatial localization of pre-rRNAs1,5, which prompted
A
62 us to examine their processing dynamics within the nucleolar substructures.
R
63 Intriguingly, unlike the multi-layered mammalian nucleolus, anamniotes such as
E
64 zebrafish possess a bipartite nucleolus with a single merged FC/DFC, named the
L
65 fibrillar zone (FZ)8,9. The evolutionary expansion of the 5' ETS and ITS2 regions17
E
66 implies a potential need for more efficient processing system in the multi-layered
C
67 nucleolar organization. However, it has remained unclear whether pre-rRNA spatial
C
68 processing in bipartite nucleoli differs from that in mammals, and whether this relates
A
69 to the emergence of multi-layered nucleoli.
70 Here, we dissected the spatiotemporal processing of pre-rRNA intermediates,
71 revealing an unexpected segregation of SSU and LSU pre-rRNAs in human nucleoli.
3

73 5' ETS-centered SSU processing was key for maintaining nested FC/DFC subdomains.
