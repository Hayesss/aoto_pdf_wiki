## 方法概述

#### *Drosophila* genetics

Flies were housed in a temperature-controlled incubator with 12:12 h on/off light cycle at 5 25°C. *OK371-GAL4* (Bl 26160), *ppk-GAL4* (Bl 32079), *Act5C-GAL4* (Bl 4414), UASdS6KSTDETE (Bl 6914) and UAS-pAbp (Bl 9148) were obtained from the Bloomington Stock Center (Bloomington, IN), and UAS-dPERK-RNAi (VDRC 110278/KK) and UAS-d4EBP-RNAi (VDRC 35440/GD) were obtained from the Vienna *Drosophila* Resource Center (VDRC). For imaging of Class IV da sensory neurons, *ppk-GAL4* was recombined with *ppk-CD4-tdGFP* 10 (*25*). For the longevity assays, the *tub-GAL4* (Bl 5138) driver was combined with a ubiquitously expressed temperature-sensitive GAL80 under control of the alphaTub84B promoter (Bl 7019).
