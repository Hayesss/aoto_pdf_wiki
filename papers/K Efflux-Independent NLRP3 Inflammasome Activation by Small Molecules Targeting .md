## 背景与目的

Inflammasomes are cytoplasmic complexes that control the bioactivity of interleukin-1 (IL-1) family members such as IL-1b by activating the protease caspase-1 ([Schroder and](#page-13-0) [Tschopp, 2010\)](#page-13-0). These potent pro-inflammatory cytokines promote protective inflammatory responses in the context of infection, but their dysregulation can lead to pathological inflammation. Although some inflammasomes are triggered by direct binding to pathogen components within the cytoplasm, the NLRP3 inflammasome instead appears to respond to cellular stress signals triggered by structurally diverse pathogens, endogenous danger signals, and environmental irritants ([Gross](#page-12-0) [et al., 2011](#page-12-0)). Despite over a decade of research and overwhelming evidence of a role for NLRP3 in various auto-inflammatory diseases, the precise mechanism of NLRP3 inflammasome activation remains unresolved. A first signal, termed priming, triggers NF-kB-dependent upregulation of NLRP3 and pro-IL-1b expression and also lowers the activation threshold of NLRP3 by additional transcription-independent means ([Juliana](#page-13-1) [et al., 2012; Schroder and Tschopp, 2010\)](#page-13-1). Many factors can trigger subsequent NLRP3 activation, including extracellular ATP, potassium (K<sup>+</sup> ) ionophores, crystals, insoluble particles, and certain pathogens. ROS, K<sup>+</sup> efflux, and endolysosomal leakage drive NLRP3 activation by these diverse agents ([Gross](#page-12-0) [et al., 2011\)](#page-12-0). The importance of ROS in NLRP3 activation has been questioned (Mun˜ [oz-Planillo et al., 2013\)](#page-13-2), and endolysosomal leakage is selectively involved in NLRP3 activation by particles and crystals ([Hornung et al., 2008\)](#page-13-3). In contrast, it is widely accepted that K+ efflux is a universal requirement for NLRP3 activation (Mun˜ [oz-Planillo et al., 2013](#page-13-2)). ## 主要发现

### Imiquimod and CL097 Activate NLRP3 to Trigger ASC Oligomerization, IL-1 Secretion, and Pyroptosis

Previous studies have shown that the TLR7 agonist imiquimod can activate the NLRP3 inflammasome ([Kanneganti et al.,](#page-13-7) [2006](#page-13-7)). To determine whether this activity is unique to imiquimod or shared by other TLR agonists, we primed bone-marrowderived dendritic cells (BMDCs) with lipopolysaccharide (LPS) and then treated with agonists of various other TLRs, and with control inflammasome activators. While all TLR agonists triggered tumor necrosis factor (TNF) secretion from unprimed cells as expected, only imiquimod and the related imidazoquinoline CL097 activated the inflammasome, as indicated by their ability to trigger secretion of IL-1b, IL-1a, and caspase-1 in LPS-primed cells ([Figures 1A](#page-3-0)–1E). The kinetics of IL-1 secretion induced by imiquimod and CL097 were similar to that of ATP and nigericin, which are rapid activators of NLRP3 [\(Figure 1](#page-3-0)E). In contrast, R848 from various commercial sources did not activate the inflammasome in LPS-primed BMDCs or bone-marrow-derived macrophages (BMDMs) [\(Figures 1](#page-3-0)A–1D, S1A–S1D), despite a superior ability to induce TNF and other NF-kB-dependent cytokines [\(Figures 1B](#page-3-0) and 1D) ([Hemmi et al., 2002](#page-13-5)). Other imidazoquinoline and nucleic acid ligands of endosomal TLRs also failed to induce IL-1b secretion [\(Figures 1A](#page-3-0)–1D, S1A–S1D). This suggests that TLR7 activation is insufficient for inflammasome activation by imidazoquinolines.

Deficiency of NLRP3 or of the adaptor protein ASC prevented IL-1bcleavage and secretion in response to imiquimod [\(Figure 1F](#page-3-0)) ([Kanneganti et al., 2006\)](#page-13-7). Imiquimod triggered NLRP3-dependent ASC oligomerization and formation of ''specks'' [\(Figures](#page-3-0) [1](#page-3-0)G and 1H), and it also triggered pyroptotic release of lactate dehydrogenase (LDH) ([Figure 1I](#page-3-0)).

## 讨论与结论

K<sup>+</sup> efflux is widely accepted to be a universal requirement for NLRP3 inflammasome activation. Indeed, canonical activation of NLRP3 by ATP, nigericin, and particles (Mun˜ [oz-Planillo](#page-13-2) [et al., 2013\)](#page-13-2), as well as non-canonical activation via intracellular LPS and caspase-11 (Ru¨ [hl and Broz, 2015](#page-13-11)), requires K<sup>+</sup> efflux for NLRP3 activation. Here we report that imiquimod and CL097 activate NLRP3 independent of K+ efflux, demonstrating that K<sup>+</sup> efflux is not a universal requirement for NLRP3 activation. Nonetheless, imiquimod induces all the classical downstream effects of NLRP3 activation, including ASC oligomerization, pyroptosis, and secretion of IL-1b.

A role for ROS in NLRP3 activation has been proposed almost a decade ago, but a consensus on the source of ROS and mechanistic data explaining how ROS might activate NLRP3 are lacking. Imiquimod and CL097 are robust inducers of ROS, and activate NLRP3 in a ROS-dependent manner. This led us to postulate that they might have specific protein targets that account for their ability to produce ROS. We identified the flavincontaining quinone oxidoreductases NQO2 and mitochondrial Complex I as molecular targets of imiquimod and CL097.
