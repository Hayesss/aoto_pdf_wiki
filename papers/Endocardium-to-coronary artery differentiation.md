## 背景与目的

Ischemic heart disease—the leading cause of death—commonly results from coronary artery occlusions and can lead to myocardial infarction (MI) and heart failure ([Dunbar et al., 2018\)](#page-15-0). One therapeutic approach is to rebuild damaged coronary blood vessels. However, developing an intervention that regenerates coronary vasculature has proven difficult ([Yl](#page-16-0)a[-Herttuala et al., 2017](#page-16-0) € ), likely due to our poor understanding of how cardiac blood vessels develop and respond to injury.

During development, most coronary vessel endothelial cells (CV ECs) arise from two spatially distinct cell types: ECs from the sinus venosus (SV), the venous inflow of heart, and endocardium (endo) ([Red-Horse et al., 2010;](#page-16-1) [Wu et al., 2012;](#page-16-2) [Chen et al.,](#page-15-1) [2014;](#page-15-1) [Tian et al., 2013](#page-16-3), [2014](#page-16-4); [Sharma et al., 2017](#page-16-5); [Su et al., 2018;](#page-16-6) [Zhang et al., 2016](#page-16-7)). The consensus is that sprouting angiogenesis from SV and endo contribute to complementary regions of the vasculature ([Tian et al., 2013,](#page-16-3) [2014;](#page-16-4) [Sharma et al., 2017;](#page-16-8) [Chen et al., 2014](#page-15-1)), which are driven by different signaling pathways, epicardial VEGF-C/ELA versus intramyocardial VEGFA, respectively ([Wu et al., 2012](#page-16-2); [Chen et al., 2014](#page-15-1); [Sharma et al.,](#page-16-8) [2017;](#page-16-8) [Neal et al., 2019\)](#page-15-2). The presence of dual progenitors for CV ECs has several benefits. It ensures fast vascularization of the growing heart [\(Rhee et al., 2018\)](#page-16-9) and provides alternative progenitors when angiogenesis from one source is abrogated [\(Sharma et al., 2017](#page-16-8)).

## Differentiation of endo-to-CV ECs is largely restricted to early heart development

Endo cells are known to produce CV ECs that first appear during mid-gestation embryogenesis through angiogenic sprouting and continue to increase in number during postnatal life. One unanswered question is at what stages during embryogenesis does cell fate conversion occur. To address this question, we sought to perform a time course of endo labeling to determine the exact window of time at which CV ECs are lineage traced from endo progenitors.

First, we needed to obtain and characterize a Tamoxifen-inducible *Cre* line that was specific for endo cells. Arterial labeling should not influence the following experiments since previous studies have established that artery ECs are not CV progenitors ([Mikawa and Fischman,](#page-15-6) [1992\)](#page-15-6). ## 讨论与结论

Endo cells lining the heart chambers produce multiple cells in the heart, including CV ECs, mural cells, and valve mesenchyme [\(Zhang et al., 2018](#page-16-10)). Its intrinsic plasticity may offer a therapeutic approach for heart repair. Although the contribution of the endoto-CVs has been studied and debated, the underlying molecular mechanisms and whether endo retains angiogenic potential throughout development remained largely unknown.
