## 讨论与结论

Historically, therapeutic advancement in the treatment of solid tumors has been carried out in a standard sequence: novel treatments are added onto the framework of the existing standard of care in a stepwise manner. This practice, while successful with conventional, ablative, targeted, and cytotoxic oncologic therapies, warrants re-evaluation in the face of the new immune oncology landscape. Immunotherapy fundamentally differs at the mechanistic level from the current standard-of-care conventional therapies.
