## 方法概述

## **Mice**

All animal procedures were conducted according to the National Institutes of Health (NIH) Guide for the Care and Use of Laboratory Animals, under Animal Study Proposal no. 23-042 approved by the NIH Animal Care and Use Committee. C57BL/6J mice were obtained from the Jackson Laboratory. *Tet1fl/fl*, *Tet2fl/fl* and *Tet3fl/fl* (Tet triple floxed); *Pcp2-cre* mice were generated as previously described37. *Tdgfl/fl*;*Ubc-creERT2*;*Rosa26-H2B-eGFP* mice were generated as previously described38. Tet triple floxed;*Ubc-creERT2;Rosa26- LSL-H2b-eGFP* mice were generated as previously described. *Tdgfl/fl; Pcp2-cre;Rosa26-H2B-eGFP* mice were generated by intercrossing. Mice were housed under a 12-h light–dark cycle at room temperatures maintained between 20 and 26 °C and a relative humidity of 30–70%. For cytarabine administration, cytarabine (Sigma-Aldrich, C6645) was dissolved in PBS and the pH was adjusted to 7.0 using NaOH. Unless otherwise indicated, mice aged approximately 1 month were administered 2,000 mg kg–1 cytarabine by intraperitoneal injection 3–4 h before euthanasia. To induce *Tdg* knockout, mice received six consecutive daily doses of tamoxifen (150 mg kg–1 solubilized in corn oil with 10% ethanol, Sigma-Aldrich, T5648) by intraperitoneal injection, followed by a 5-day rest period before experiments. For the isolation of embryonic cortical neurons, timed pregnancies were set up using CreERT2-positive mice and CreERT2-negative mice for both *Tdg–/–* and *Tet–l–* mice. Vaginal plug detection was performed the following morning to confirm the presence of a plug.


## 讨论与结论

The antimetabolite cytarabine was approved by the US Food and Drug Administration in 1969 for the treatment of leukaemia and has remained the mainstay of acute myeloid leukaemia (AML) chemotherapy. Although adult patients who receive high-dose cytarabine have a higher probability of relapse-free survival than patients who receive low-dose regimens, 10–25% of patients receiving high-dose cytarabine develop cerebellar dysfunction39, which limits successful treatment outcomes.

Biochemical reconstitution of active TET–TDG-mediated DNA demethylation indicated a highly coordinated and sequential cytosine oxidation–BER reaction in which demethylation-associated repair on one strand is completed before demethylation begins on the symmetrically opposite 5mC40. As such, incorporation of cytosine analogue chemotherapeutics during demethylation-initiated BER can potentially lead to abortive repair and persistent DNA nicks unless they can be efficiently ligated by XRCC1–LIG3. Although gemcitabine and cytarabine are both readily incorporated by Polβ during demethylation-initiated BER41, XRCC1–LIG3-dependent ligation was more efficient for dFdCTP than for AraCTP. Consequently, gemcitabine produced fewer SSBs and rarely DSBs and was not toxic to neurons. We therefore propose that gemcitabine could be a safer alternative chemotherapeutic agent for AML treatment without some of the harsh side effects of high-dose cytarabine. In this context, it is notable that a previous screen demonstrated that gemcitabine is more potent than cytarabine in paediatric AML both in vitro and in vivo.

In contrast to dFdCTP, when AraCTP was incorporated into DNA by Polβ during demethylation-initiated BER, a DSB formed, which triggered the DNA damage response and the rapid activation of genes that respond to stress and in turn potentially cell death or senescence.
