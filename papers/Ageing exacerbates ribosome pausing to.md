## 方法概述

#### **Strains and growth conditions**

The Bristol N2 strain of *C. elegans* was grown at 20 °C on nematode growth medium agar plates seeded with *Escherichia coli* strain OP50 according to standard methods. All yeast experiments were performed using derivatives of BY4741 (*MATa his3Δ1 leu2Δ0 met15Δ0 ura3Δ0*). Ribo-seq experiments used diploid BY4743 (WT) and homozygous *sch9Δ/sch9Δ* (YSC6275-201917395), which were obtained from GE Healthcare Dharmacon. For immunoblotting and microscopy experiments, haploid strains of WT, *ltn1Δ*, *rqc2Δ, hel2Δ* and *sch9Δ* were from the Yeast Knockout Collection[68.](#page-8-1) Strains from the N-terminally GFP tagged seamless collectio[n69](#page-8-2) as well as from the C-terminally GFP tagged clone collection were used for the *YTM1* and *HAT2* experiments. Yeast cells were grown at 30 °C in variations of synthetic complete (SC) medium with a fivefold excess of nutrients to compensate for strain auxotrophies.
